About Me

I'm a data science graduate student who wants to work in business analytics and consulting, helping organizations make better decisions with data.

To me, problem solving and analysis aren't just strengths. They're the part of the work I enjoy most. I like taking a messy question, finding the right data, and working through it until the answer is clear enough to act on.

My background in sociology taught me to ask why people and organizations behave the way they do. Data science gives me the tools to answer that with evidence. Leading teams on and off the lacrosse field taught me how to turn analysis into action that people actually follow.

Outside of coursework, I spend a lot of time reading and researching all kinds of topics. That curiosity carries straight into my work, because every new dataset is another chance to learn something.

Capstone Project · Interactive Dashboard

AI Dependence & the Trajectory of Data-Driven Business Decisions

Research question: How has the dependency on AI changed the trajectory for data-driven business decisions, and what role does it play in shaping how organizations rely on it?

This dashboard compares evidence on four areas: the job market and hiring, business use of AI, financial outcomes, and data literacy. Each area weighs positive and negative findings from government, academic and industry sources side by side to limit research bias.
